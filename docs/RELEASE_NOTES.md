TCG Alerts v0.8.0

- **Decks → Find missing in stores** searches your synced store catalogues for every missing card. You pick the listings, and the app links and tracks them in one step.
- **Riftbound deck checks**: 40-card main deck including the Champion, 1 Legend, 12 runes, 3 different battlefields, up to 3 copies of a card across the main deck and sideboard, and a sideboard of up to 10.
- **Calendar**: release dates the publisher states in news text, such as "launches worldwide on October 23, 2026", are added automatically as Tentative entries with the article linked. Reminders fire 7 days, 1 day and 0 days before each dated release.
- **Store catalogues** re-sync automatically every 12 hours while the app is open.
- **Linux desktop notifications** through notify-send.
- **Layout**: the Settings and Discover pages now scroll, so backup and restore are reachable, and the sidebar logo is no longer clipped.
